Reasonable suspicions

What does “free to start” actually mean?

Every feature listed on this page is free right now, with no credit card, no trial period, and no usage cap. It is not a limited tier of a paid product; it is the product.

Will you start charging?

Possibly, for the AI advisor, because running a language model costs money per question. Nothing that is free today is planned to move behind a paywall, and any change would be announced on the public changelog before it took effect.

Why is there no price for Pro?

Because there is no Pro. Advertising a price for something that does not exist yet, or a trial for a tier nobody can use, would be inventing a product. When there is something to charge for, there will be a price here.

How do you make money right now?

We do not. It is built in the open by one developer, and the running costs are small because there is no bank-aggregation vendor to pay. There are no ads and your data is not sold.
