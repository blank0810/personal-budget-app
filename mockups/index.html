Landing redesign — three directions

Static mockups. Same copy spine and the same real screenshot in each, so what you are comparing is the feel: palette, typography, density, and how loud the page is willing to be. Nothing here is wired to the app.

  1. A · Plain Statement

    Bone canvas, near-black ink, one signal red-orange spent sparingly. Hairline evidence rows instead of cards. Loud through scale and contrast, not colour — the marketing face of the app's own Health Ledger.

    The surface is the colour. Deep oxblood carries most of the page and the product screenshot is the only bright object on it, so your data is the thing that glows. Gold reserved for figures.
