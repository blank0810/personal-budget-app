title: Say What the Adjustment Was
status: current
description: >
  Correcting an account balance no longer files an anonymous "Manual Balance
  Adjustment". You can say what the money actually went to, file it under a
  category, and count it against the month's envelope — so the gap between
  what you logged and what you spent stops being a mystery.
features:
  - title: Balance Adjustments
    items:
      - "**Say what it was.** An optional description replaces the generic label, so a reconciliation reads like the thing that actually happened. Leave it blank and it stays 'Manual Balance Adjustment'."
      - "**File it properly.** Pick a category and the adjustment lands in your reports where it belongs instead of pooling in 'Initial Balance/Adjustment'. The list follows the direction of the change — income categories when the balance goes up, expense categories when it goes down."
      - "**Count it against a budget.** Cash you spent but never logged can now consume the envelope it really came from, so the remaining amount reflects reality. Choosing a budget fills in its category for you."
      - "**Errors come back to you.** The dialog now shows what went wrong instead of failing quietly."
  - title: Under the Hood
    items:
      - "**Tithe and emergency fund stay out of it.** Reconciling a balance never triggers those transfers, even when you file the adjustment under a real income category."
      - "**A budget can only take what belongs to it.** Adjustments are checked against the account, the category and the envelope before anything is written."
      - "**Faster account pages.** The ledger no longer loads every budgeted expense just to draw a dropdown."
