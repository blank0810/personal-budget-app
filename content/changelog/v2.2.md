title: Clearer Financial Health, More Flexible Invoicing
status: current
description: >
  See where your finances stand and keep invoice tracking true to what happened
  outside the app. The redesigned Health Ledger puts your score, verdict, five
  health pillars, supporting evidence, and most important next action into one
  direct view. Invoices can now be marked as sent or paid without sending an
  email, while invoice and receipt delivery remain available when you choose
  them. Reports stays focused on deeper analysis, and Transaction Statements
  now line up cleanly from opening balance to closing balance.
features:
  - title: A Dashboard That Gives You an Answer
    items:
      - "**Your financial position comes first.** When enough data is available, the dashboard opens with your health score, a plain-spoken verdict, your weakest area, and one action that can actually improve it."
      - "**Five questions expose the whole picture.** The new Health Ledger grades solvency, liquidity, savings, debt management, and cash flow in aligned rows, with the real evidence behind every grade."
      - "**No fake certainty.** If your finances do not contain enough information for a fair diagnosis, the dashboard says what is missing instead of handing you a misleading score."
  - title: Evidence, Not Decoration
    items:
      - "**The numbers that matter are visible at a glance.** Net worth, current-month income, expenses, and surplus or deficit sit in one compact evidence strip."
      - "**The detail follows the diagnosis.** Six-month cash flow, budget pressure, accounts and debt, and recent activity now support the verdict in a clear reading order."
      - "**Less motion, more meaning.** The account carousel, animated counters, drawing score ring, looping AI teaser, and staggered entrance effects are gone."
  - title: Quick Actions, Kept Close
    items:
      - "**The useful shortcuts stayed.** Add income, add an expense, transfer money, or pay debt directly from the dashboard without turning the page into another widget collection."
      - "**Every recommendation leads somewhere real.** Health Ledger actions open an existing workflow or take you to the relevant part of the app; there are no decorative buttons or invented targets."
  - title: Invoice Status Without Mandatory Email
    items:
      - "**Record what happened, even when it happened outside the app.** Mark a draft invoice as sent without emailing it, so hand-delivered or separately shared invoices still have the right status."
      - "**Email only when you choose to.** Invoice and paid-receipt delivery are optional and start unchecked each time; the choice appears when the client has an email address."
      - "**Payment tracking stays accurate.** Mark a sent or overdue invoice as paid with the payment date, with or without sending a receipt."
      - "**A delivery problem does not undo your work.** If a selected email cannot be sent, the new status stays saved and you can retry delivery from the invoice."
  - title: Reports Without Repetition
    items:
      - "**The duplicate Overview is gone.** Financial health, summary cards, and the net-worth overview now live where they belong: on the dashboard."
      - "**Reports starts with analysis.** Income & Expenses, Budget Analytics, and Statements remain available for the deeper work the dashboard should not try to duplicate."
      - "**Transaction Statements line up properly.** Date, Description, Category, Budget Status, Amount, and Balance now use evenly spaced columns, with opening and closing balances aligned to the same grid."
